### Business outcome
Sam reported **6 WhatsApp clicks/enquiries from the website this month**. Treat this as the current commercial baseline supplied by Sam. Future reviews should compare website enquiries as well as search metrics.

### Strategy decisions
- Article-first SEO/GEO acquisition.
- Project Pages are mainly sales/conversion funnel pages; ranking them is optional, not the main KPI.
- Do not pre-label articles as money content. Let Search Console and enquiry behaviour identify winners.
- Use evergreen topics/URLs by default; avoid unnecessary year-based titles/URLs.
- Validate keywords/topics with Search Console + demand/Trend signals + live SERP competition + buyer intent.
- Use simple secondary-school-level English.
- Fact-check material facts before publishing; never invent uncertain facts.
- Internal links only by default. No clickable developer/portal/news/government/third-party links unless Sam explicitly approves. Sam's own YouTube is an allowed exception when provided/approved.
- Research external sources when needed; source names may be stated in plain text without outbound links.
