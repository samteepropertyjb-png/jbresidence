The Kews Iskandar Puteri: Is This Landed Location Right for You?

Sam Tee Property Advisor · REN 80322
September 2026

When buyers look for an Iskandar Puteri landed property, they often start with familiar names such as Eco Botanic, Sunway Iskandar and Horizon Hills. The Kews deserves a place on the shortlist for a different reason: it offers a more residential setting near the Malaysia–Singapore Second Link. The real question is whether that setting suits your daily life.

In brief: The Kews may suit buyers who value a calmer landed environment and can drive for shopping and dining. Buyers who want commercial activity close to home may prefer Eco Botanic; those organising family life around a school may find Sunway Iskandar more practical. Visit each area before deciding.

Where is The Kews?

The Kews is a landed residential development in Iskandar Puteri, in the western Johor corridor served by the Second Link. Kew Green and Kew Woods are product names within The Kews. The project sits near Leisure Farm, an established high-end residential area. They are separate developments; The Kews is not a Leisure Farm phase or address.

The developer's location information identifies the Sultan Abu Bakar CIQ Complex, the Second Link, Eco Galleria, Sunway Iskandar, schools and healthcare as regional reference points. That gives buyers options across Iskandar Puteri, but it does not mean each destination is within walking distance. For everyday plans, check the route from the actual entrance and the times you expect to travel.

Why does Second Link connectivity matter?

For someone who regularly drives to Singapore through Tuas, a landed property near Second Link can be worth exploring. Living in this part of Iskandar Puteri may make the western crossing more relevant than it would be from a home in central Johor Bahru. It can also appeal to households whose work, school or family routines already take them towards western Johor.

That is a location advantage, not a guaranteed commute time. Traffic, immigration queues, tolls and the journey beyond the checkpoint all matter. Test the full trip during your usual weekday hours. If your main destination is in central Singapore and you rely on public transport, compare the whole journey with other Johor locations before treating proximity to the crossing as decisive.

The Kews vs Eco Botanic vs Sunway Iskandar

These locations offer different ways to live. The comparison is less about which name sounds more prestigious and more about what you want outside your front door.

Eco Botanic: convenience and activity

Eco Galleria and other commercial areas give Eco Botanic a stronger shopping, dining and service presence. If you like having more daily choices and a livelier setting nearby, that is a clear benefit. The trade-off is that busy commercial areas may feel less restful to someone seeking a quiet retreat.

Sunway Iskandar: integrated family living

Sunway City Iskandar Puteri is planned as an integrated township with retail, recreation and Sunway International School. Its school and family ecosystem may appeal to parents who want those routines within the same broader township. It may feel more active than the residential atmosphere a quiet-lifestyle buyer is seeking.

The Kews: a more residential setting

The Kews is worth considering if you would rather return to a calmer residential environment and drive out for most shopping, cafés and errands. Its appeal is the balance of landed living and access to the Second Link, rather than a dense commercial centre on the doorstep. The exact feel will vary by street, surrounding construction and time of day.

Compare Sunway City, Horizon Hills and Eco Botanic in more detail if you are still deciding which township character fits you.

Is The Kews suitable for retirement living?

It could be a sensible option for someone planning semi-retirement or retirement who wants to slow down, enjoy a landed home and keep busy retail activity at some distance. A quieter home base can be attractive when there is less need to live beside a workplace or school. It can also work for a household with adult children that wants room for visits without moving into a more commercial township.

Retirement fit requires a more practical check than a peaceful impression at a viewing. Consider whether you are comfortable driving for groceries, dining, healthcare and social visits. Ask how much garden and house maintenance you want to manage. Check stairs, a ground-floor bedroom if needed, and how easily family can reach you. Our Johor retirement property checklist can help structure that visit.

Is The Kews worth considering?

Yes, if a newer landed home in a less commercial setting is genuinely high on your list. The Kews gives buyers another option beyond the best-known townships. The developer presents Kew Green and Kew Woods within its product range, but the right comparison is the particular home available to you, its price and the lifestyle around it, not a broad project label.

The main trade-off is convenience. If your family wants to walk to cafés and shops, Eco Botanic may be easier to enjoy. If daily school travel drives the decision, Sunway Iskandar may deserve a closer look. Buyers who prefer an established neighbourhood with a wider subsale selection can also compare Horizon Hills and other mature landed areas. None of these choices is right for every household.

Before committing, visit at a weekday morning, an evening and a weekend. Drive your real routes, look at nearby construction and inspect the specific home. Compare the full purchase and ownership costs, not only the asking price. Current availability, packages and floor plans should be verified for the exact unit.

Sam's view

I would show The Kews first to a buyer who values peace at home, is comfortable relying on a car, and uses the Second Link corridor often enough for that location to matter. I would also include it for someone exploring a slower, semi-retirement lifestyle in Iskandar Puteri.

For a family that prioritises walkable conveniences or a particular school, I would start by comparing Eco Botanic or Sunway Iskandar. The best shortlist comes from your routine, budget and future plans. If you are still setting a price range, my Iskandar Puteri landed property budget guide is a useful next step.
