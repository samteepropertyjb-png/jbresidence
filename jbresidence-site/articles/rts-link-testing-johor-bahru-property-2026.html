RTS Link Has Entered Testing: Which Johor Bahru Properties Could Benefit?

Sam Tee Property Advisor · REN 80322
September 2026

The Johor Bahru–Singapore RTS Link has entered testing and commissioning. Test trains are running and supporting transport measures are being prepared, but public passenger service has not started. For buyers, this is the point to separate genuine connectivity from loose use of the word “RTS” in property marketing.

Current position: testing and trial runs are under way. Completion is expected around the end of 2026, with public operations around the beginning of 2027, subject to final testing and official confirmation.

What the RTS Link will actually connect

The cross-border line connects Bukit Chagar in central Johor Bahru with Woodlands North in Singapore. The train journey is expected to take approximately five minutes, with Malaysian and Singapore immigration procedures completed at the departure station.

Five minutes is not the complete commute. A resident must still reach Bukit Chagar, clear immigration, wait for the train and continue from Woodlands North. The most relevant properties are therefore those with a practical route to the station—not simply projects located somewhere in Johor Bahru.

Three levels of RTS property exposure

1. Walkable central-JB properties

Homes with a realistic walking route to Bukit Chagar have the most direct connection. They may suit Malaysians working in Singapore, frequent cross-border travellers and tenants who prefer not to rely on a car.

Buyers should personally test the route. A map does not show pedestrian crossings, covered access, construction barriers, heat or rain. R&F Princess Cove is one central-JB option worth comparing because of its position near the CIQ and city-centre transport network. Suitability still depends on the phase, unit price, management and intended use.

2. Short-transfer properties in JB Town

Some developments may not be comfortably walkable but could benefit from a short bus, shuttle or ride-hailing journey. Summer Suites may appeal to buyers seeking a city-centre property with dual-key flexibility.

The important question is not whether a brochure says “near RTS”, but whether the complete transfer to Bukit Chagar remains reliable during commuting hours. Buyers should rely only on confirmed feeder services and operating details.

3. Wider Johor properties with indirect benefits

Properties farther away may benefit indirectly from stronger business activity and confidence surrounding the RTS and the Johor–Singapore Special Economic Zone. But homes in Iskandar Puteri, Medini or near the Second Link are not directly served by this RTS line.

For someone working in Tuas or Jurong, the Second Link may remain more practical than travelling east to Bukit Chagar before returning to western Singapore.

Will RTS automatically increase rental returns?

No. The RTS can enlarge the potential tenant pool and make cross-border travel more predictable, but rental performance still depends on actual door-to-door time, rent, unit condition, management, maintenance charges, competing supply and last-mile transport.

If many investors purchase similar units for rent, additional supply may limit rent increases even as demand improves. Rental projections should be treated as scenarios, not promises.

Is it already too late to buy before RTS?

Not necessarily, but buyers are no longer entering before the market knows about the project. RTS has appeared in property marketing for years, and some expected benefits may already be reflected in asking prices.

Instead of asking only whether RTS will increase prices, compare completed transactions, achievable rents, management quality and the premium already included in the price. Good infrastructure cannot correct an unsuitable layout, excessive maintenance fee or weak building management.

What buyers should verify

  1. Travel from the property to Bukit Chagar during peak hours.
  2. Check whether the route is genuinely walkable in heat and rain.
  3. Confirm that any shuttle or covered link is operational, not merely proposed.
  4. Compare recent transactions and current rental evidence.
  5. Calculate maintenance, furnishing and vacancy costs.
  6. Check the title, tenure, developer information and sale terms.
  7. Avoid guaranteed-return or guaranteed-appreciation claims.

Foreign buyers should also calculate transfer duty, Johor foreign-interest approval charges and legal expenses. Start with the Johor foreign-buyer cost guide.

Sam's take

The testing phase matters because it reduces uncertainty over whether the infrastructure will materialise. But the strongest RTS-related property is not automatically the project using “RTS” most aggressively in its advertising.

Look for a sensibly priced, well-managed home with a practical route to Bukit Chagar and a genuine owner-occupier or tenant use case. The train should strengthen the property’s logic—not become the only reason to buy.

目前情况：系统测试和试运行正在进行。项目预计于2026年底左右完成，并在通过最终测试后，于2027年初左右投入载客服务，确切日期仍以官方公布为准。

RTS实际连接哪里？

RTS连接新山市中心的武吉查卡与新加坡兀兰北。两站之间的列车行程预计约五分钟，乘客将在出发站完成马来西亚和新加坡两国的出入境手续。

五分钟并不是完整通勤时间。居民仍需前往武吉查卡、通关、候车，并从兀兰北继续前往最终目的地。因此，真正相关的是能够方便前往车站的房地产，而不是所有位于新山的项目。

三种RTS受益程度

一、可步行前往武吉查卡

拥有实际步行路线的住宅具备最直接的连接，可能适合在新加坡工作的马来西亚人、经常跨境人士及不希望依赖汽车的租客。

买家应亲自测试步行路线。地图不能反映行人过路处、有盖通道、建筑障碍及日晒雨淋。R&F富力公主湾是可纳入比较的新山市中心项目，但是否适合仍取决于具体期数、价格、管理及购买用途。

二、需要短程接驳的新山市区住宅

部分项目可能不适合步行，但可通过短程巴士、接驳车或电召车前往武吉查卡。Summer Suites可能适合希望进入市中心市场并重视双钥匙灵活性的买家。

真正应该检查的不是宣传资料有没有写“靠近RTS”，而是繁忙时段的完整接驳是否稳定。任何接驳服务都应以已经确认的路线及营运资料为准。

三、间接受益的其他柔佛地区

较远地区可能因RTS及柔新经济特区带来的商业活动和市场信心而间接受益。不过，依斯干达公主城、Medini或第二通道附近的住宅并没有直接连接这条RTS。

如果买家在大士或裕廊工作，使用第二通道可能比先前往武吉查卡更加实际。

RTS会自动提高租金回报吗？

不会。RTS可能扩大愿意居住在柔佛并前往新加坡工作的租客群体，但实际出租表现仍取决于完整通勤时间、租金、单位状况、大楼管理、管理费、竞争供应及最后一段交通。

如果大量投资者购买相似单位出租，新增供应也可能限制租金上涨。未来租金只能作为评估情景，不能当作保证。

现在购买是否已经太迟？

不一定，但市场早已知道RTS的存在。RTS已被房地产项目宣传多年，部分预期可能已经反映在叫价中。

不要只问RTS是否会推高房价。还应比较近期成交、当前真实租金、大楼管理，以及售价已经包含多少RTS溢价。良好的基础设施不能弥补不实用的格局、过高的管理费或较差的管理。

购买前应核实什么？

  1. 在繁忙时段实际测试从住宅前往武吉查卡的路线。
  2. 检查炎热或下雨时是否仍适合步行。
  3. 确认接驳车或有盖通道已经营运，而不只是建议。
  4. 比较近期成交和当前实际租金。
  5. 计算管理费、家具及空置成本。
  6. 检查地契、产权、发展商资料及销售条款。
  7. 避免任何保证回报或保证升值的说法。

外国买家还应计算转让印花税、柔佛外国权益批准费用及律师费。可先阅读2026年柔佛外国买家费用指南。

Sam的看法

RTS进入测试阶段确实重要，因为它进一步降低了基础设施无法落实的不确定性。但最值得考虑的RTS房地产，并不一定是广告中最常使用“RTS”字眼的项目。

应选择价格合理、管理良好、前往武吉查卡方便，并拥有真实自住或租赁需求的住宅。RTS应该加强项目本身的逻辑，而不是成为唯一的购买理由。
