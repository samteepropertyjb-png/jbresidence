International Schools Near Iskandar Puteri: 2026 Guide

Sam Tee Property Advisor · REN 80322
Iskandar Puteri, Johor

For many families moving to Iskandar Puteri, the school decision comes before the home search. The area has British, American, Canadian/IB and other international-school options. This guide explains the main choices and the practical questions to consider when choosing where to live.

Why Iskandar Puteri became an international school hub

EduCity and the wider Iskandar Puteri area bring several international schools into one part of Johor. This gives families a real choice of curriculum and school setting. It can also make the daily school run easier than living far away in another part of Johor Bahru.

That does not mean every school or home suits every family. A curriculum, available year group, transport plan and admission place should be checked directly with the school before a family chooses a property.

The key international schools in and around Iskandar Puteri

Marlborough College Malaysia (Edu City)

Marlborough College Malaysia is a British boarding and day school in Iskandar Puteri for pupils aged 3 to 18. Its senior pathway includes IGCSE and the IB Diploma. It may be relevant for families who want a British-style school experience or who are considering boarding as well as day schooling.

Raffles American School (Iskandar Puteri)

Raffles American School serves children from pre-kindergarten to Grade 12. Its American-style programme and Advanced Placement options may be worth exploring for families who expect their child to continue on a US-focused pathway. The school is on Jalan Raffles in Iskandar Puteri.

Sunway International School (Sunway City Iskandar Puteri)

Sunway International School is within Sunway City Iskandar Puteri and serves Kindergarten to Grade 12. It offers a combination of the Canadian curriculum and International Baccalaureate programmes. This can be useful for families whose work, home and daily needs are also on the Sunway side of Iskandar Puteri.

Invictus International School (Horizon Hills)

Invictus International School has a Horizon Hills campus. It is another option for families who prefer to be closer to Horizon Hills and its surrounding neighbourhoods. Visit the campus and confirm the available year group, curriculum and transport arrangement for your child.

IDRISSI International School (EduCity)

IDRISSI has a campus in EduCity Iskandar Puteri, with preschool, primary and secondary levels. Families who value its nature-based and values-led approach may wish to include it in their school visits.

Which areas are practical for the school run?

Location still matters, but a map does not show the full daily routine. These are broad starting points, not promises of a fixed journey time:

  • Eco Botanic is often considered by families looking at schools around EduCity and the wider Nusajaya side.
  • Horizon Hills may suit families considering the Horizon Hills school area or a more established landed-home setting.
  • Puteri Harbour can appeal to families who prefer waterfront apartments or a more urban setting, while remaining in Iskandar Puteri.
  • Sunway Iskandar is a natural place to consider for a family committed to Sunway International School or a Sunway City routine.

Test the journey from the exact home to the exact school gate at the time you would normally travel. Traffic, the chosen precinct, school start time and your work route can change the result.

Fees and admission: check the current details

School fees can differ widely by year group. They can also include registration, deposits, transport, meals, activities and tax. Published fees are updated by schools from time to time, so use them only as a starting point and ask each school for a current written schedule.

Do the same for admission. A nearby home does not guarantee a place. Confirm the year-group availability, application timing, transport and any required documents before making a rental or purchase decision around a school.

Where do families usually live near international schools in Iskandar Puteri?

Families normally consider more than school distance. The right area depends on the child’s school, but also on how the whole household will live each day.

  • Daily school drop-off: A short route can make a large difference over a full school year. Check both morning and afternoon travel.
  • Home setting: Some families want a gated and guarded landed neighbourhood with more space. Others prefer an apartment with simpler upkeep.
  • Daily needs: Groceries, food, healthcare, clubs and weekend activities all affect whether an area feels practical.
  • Work travel: A family commuting to Singapore or another part of Johor may make a different location choice from a family working mainly in Iskandar Puteri.
  • Length of stay: A one-year assignment and a seven-year school plan can lead to different housing decisions.

For a useful comparison of the established landed areas, see Horizon Hills, Eco Botanic and East Ledang. It can help families separate school access from the other lifestyle trade-offs.

Rent first or buy? A family decision, not a default answer

Some families rent first when they are new to Johor. Renting can give them time to test the school run, learn the neighbourhood and make sure the school is the right fit. It may also suit a short work assignment.

If a child is likely to study in Iskandar Puteri for several years, the family may later want to compare renting with buying. Buying is not automatically the better choice. It depends on budget, how long the family expects to stay, the daily route, whether they prefer a landed home or condominium, and whether they would want to keep the property after the child leaves school.

Families exploring landed homes can start with the Iskandar Puteri landed property guide. It is a useful next step before looking at individual homes.

Property options to compare after the school choice is clear

Once the school and preferred area are clearer, compare homes based on your real family routine rather than the project name alone. For example:

  • Estuari Greens and ParkHomes 2 are landed-home options to consider if the Puteri Harbour side and home space matter to your family.
  • Ascent Park is another landed-home option for families comparing the wider Iskandar Puteri school corridor.
  • Families focused on Horizon Hills can also read the Horizon Hills resale versus new-launch guide before deciding whether a new or existing home better suits their move.

These are options to compare, not a recommendation for every family. Check the actual route to school, home layout, monthly costs and the terms of any purchase before deciding.

Plan the school and home together

The best home decision starts with the child’s school, but it should not end there. A family can have a good school choice and still find the daily route, home size or work commute difficult. Visit the school, test the journey and compare a few areas before committing.
