Buying Near JB CIQ? Why You Should Understand R&F Princess Cove Before Comparing Other Projects

Sam TeeProperty Advisor · REN 80322
September 2026

R&F Princess Cove is often one of the first projects buyers notice when they search near Johor Bahru CIQ. That does not make it the answer for everyone. It does make it worth understanding before you compare it with other city-centre condos.

Good comparisons start with daily use. A buyer who crosses the Causeway often may care about a very different set of things from a buyer who works from home in JB, has young children, or wants a quieter low-density environment.

What buyers are usually comparing

R&F Princess Cove sits in the JB Town / CIQ catchment. For frequent Causeway users, that broad location can be relevant. It is still important to test the actual route at the time you expect to travel. Traffic, weather, crossing procedures and your starting point inside the development can change the experience.

Do not assume that a project near CIQ automatically gives an easy daily commute. Compare your real door-to-door routine, including parking, lift time, any walking involved and the time you normally leave home.

1. Smaller layouts can suit a simple city base

Some R&F layouts may appeal to a single buyer or couple who wants a lower-maintenance home. A smaller unit can be easier to furnish and can leave more budget for location, holding costs and daily life.

That is not always a benefit. If two people work from home, need storage or expect family visits, a compact layout may feel restrictive. Read our 1-bedroom versus 2+1-bedroom guide before deciding whether a smaller unit is genuinely enough.

2. An integrated mall and commercial environment

For some buyers, being close to shops, food and everyday services makes a high-rise easier to live in. The R&F Mall and wider commercial environment are therefore a practical part of the comparison, not just a marketing feature.

For other buyers, a busy mixed environment can mean more activity, traffic or a lifestyle they do not want. Visit at the hours you would normally be home. Check what is open, how you would handle groceries, and whether the atmosphere suits you on a weekday as well as a weekend.

3. CIQ-oriented accessibility matters to some buyers

If you regularly use the Causeway, a JB Town location oriented toward CIQ may deserve extra weight in your shortlist. It can also make sense for an owner who wants a city base rather than a large family home.

However, CIQ is not the only transport question. Buyers thinking about the RTS should compare that station-oriented routine separately. Our CIQ versus RTS location guide explains why the two travel patterns should not be treated as the same.

When these R&F advantages may not matter to you

  • You need more bedrooms, a larger kitchen or a proper separate work area.
  • You prefer landed living, a lower-density setting or a quieter family routine.
  • Your work and daily errands are mainly in Iskandar Puteri rather than JB Town.
  • You will not use the Causeway often enough for the city location to justify the price and lifestyle trade-off.
  • You are buying mainly for rental income without a realistic tenant, furnishing and vacancy plan.

In those cases, it may be better to compare a different JB Town project or look beyond the city centre. The JB Town buyer hub is a useful starting point, while Iskandar Puteri may suit buyers who prioritise a larger family setting.

Questions to take to every viewing

  1. Which exact layout and tower am I comparing?
  2. What would my full daily route to CIQ look like at my actual travel time?
  3. Will the commercial setting make my daily life easier, or feel too busy?
  4. How much space will I use in the next three to five years?
  5. What are the management fees, furnishing needs and other recurring costs?

Sam's practical view

R&F Princess Cove can be a sensible comparison point for buyers who value a compact city layout, nearby commercial convenience and a CIQ-oriented JB Town location. It is not a universal winner. The right decision depends on how you live, how often you travel and whether the unit itself works for your future plans.
