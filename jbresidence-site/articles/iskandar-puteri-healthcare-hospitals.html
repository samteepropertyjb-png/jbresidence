Why Are Major Hospital Groups Investing in Iskandar Puteri?

Sam TeeProperty Advisor · October 2026

Short answer: Iskandar Puteri combines space for future communities, access to Singapore and potential regional healthcare demand. Major hospital investments are another reason to examine the area as a place for long-term living—not a guarantee of future population growth.

When people talk about Iskandar Puteri, they often mention new homes, international schools and its location near Singapore. But major healthcare groups are also investing here. Why? Here is my perspective as a local property agent.

Why healthcare groups are looking at Iskandar Puteri

Hospitals require long-term planning. Operators assess future patient demand, accessibility and the ability to recruit medical professionals—not only the population living nearby today. Iskandar Puteri has room for master-planned residential and commercial communities. As more homes are completed and occupied, demand for everyday services and healthcare could grow. This is a reasonable market interpretation, not a claim that hospital operators have confirmed every one of these motives.

Singapore and the retirement question

Singapore has a strong healthcare system, but some private treatments can be costly. Depending on insurance, treatment and travel needs, selected patients may consider care in Johor. The Second Link also makes western Johor accessible to drivers from Singapore, although border traffic varies. For people considering retirement, healthcare access matters alongside the home itself: specialist appointments, daily shopping, mobility and family visits should all be part of the decision. Medical travel does not necessarily mean someone will relocate to Malaysia.

A wider regional healthcare market

Healthcare demand is not limited to Singapore. Patients from Indonesia and other countries also consider treatment in Malaysia. International patients can support specialist services beyond the immediate residential population. Still, medical tourism and permanent residency are separate markets, and neither should be confused with guaranteed population growth.

What is being developed?

Gleneagles Hospital Johor, Medini

IHH Healthcare announced an expansion involving Afiniti Wellness and plans for an ambulatory care centre, with a target around 2028. Gleneagles already operates in Medini.

Sunway Medical Centre Iskandar Puteri

Sunway Healthcare has announced a new hospital in Sunway City Iskandar Puteri, with more than 400 planned beds. Published schedules and opening dates remain subject to change.

These projects would add to existing options such as Columbia Asia Hospital Iskandar Puteri and Kensington Green Specialist Centre. Future facilities should not be treated as already operating.

Why this matters to people choosing a home

Healthcare is one part of a comfortable long-term lifestyle. Iskandar Puteri also offers international schools, shopping, dining and established or growing communities. Eco Botanic, Sunway Iskandar and Bukit Indah provide different neighbourhood experiences and practical amenities. Schools such as Marlborough College Malaysia, Raffles American School and Sunway International School add options for families.

For residents who drive, the area's road network connects neighbourhoods, schools, retail areas and the Second Link. Actual travel times depend on the route, peak-hour traffic and border conditions.

For a homebuyer, the useful question is not simply whether a hospital or school exists nearby. It is whether the location supports their everyday routine now—and as their family or healthcare needs change.

My view

Iskandar Puteri is gradually becoming a more complete place to live, not simply an area with new houses. Its combination of healthcare, international schools, neighbourhood amenities and road access may appeal to families, international residents and retirees. The right neighbourhood still depends on each household's needs.
