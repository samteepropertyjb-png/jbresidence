Medini · Iskandar Puteri

Medini Condo Guide 2026: Prices, Lifestyle, Rental Demand and Who It Suits

Prices, lifestyle, rental demand and the buyer profiles that may fit this quieter side of Iskandar Puteri.

By Sam Tee  ·  Updated 6 September 2026  ·  8 min read

Medini is not simply a cheaper version of JB city centre. It serves a different type of buyer, tenant and visitor.

Why are buyers looking at Medini again?

Medini is one of the main high-rise areas in Iskandar Puteri. It sits close to LEGOLAND Malaysia, Gleneagles Hospital Johor, EduCity, Puteri Harbour, Sunway Iskandar and the Second Link direction. For buyers who want to enter Iskandar Puteri without paying the price of a new landed home, it offers many condos and serviced residences at a lower total purchase price.

Completed choices include One Medini, Grand Medini, The Elysia Park Residence, Meridin Suites, Paradiso Nuova and The M @ Medini Macrolink. Newer choices include Bodaiju Residences. Almas Suites is in nearby Puteri Harbour, not Medini itself, but buyers often include it in the same comparison.

What supports daily life in Medini?

LEGOLAND is inside Bandar Medini, while Gleneagles Hospital Johor and the Raffles University Medini campus are nearby. Marlborough College Malaysia and Raffles American School are also within the wider Iskandar Puteri area. Residents commonly drive to Mall of Medini, Eco Botanic, Puteri Harbour and Sunway Big Box for shopping and dining.

This is a more spread-out and car-dependent lifestyle than JB town. Its attractions, education and healthcare anchors can make it feel more family and leisure oriented, although that is a market positioning observation—not an official demographic statistic.

Is Medini suitable for Airbnb?

Medini has a natural short-stay story because families visit LEGOLAND and nearby attractions. Larger two- and three-bedroom layouts may suit parents travelling with children. But “near LEGOLAND” does not automatically mean profitable Airbnb.

Before buying, confirm whether short-term rental is allowed by the building’s current JMB or management corporation rules. Include furnishing, cleaning, platform fees, utilities, repairs, management and low-season vacancy when calculating net return.

Who may suit Medini?

  • Young buyers seeking a first home below the cost of many new landed launches.
  • Couples and small families who prefer more bedrooms, parking and a quieter setting.
  • Singapore-linked buyers who use the Second Link direction.
  • Investors who understand a specific tenant or guest segment.
  • Foreign buyers who have checked current eligibility with a lawyer and the relevant authority.

The risks buyers should not ignore

High-rise supply and competition matter. Inspect actual occupancy, building maintenance, lift condition, sinking fund, rental evidence, furnishing level and competing listings. A low purchase price helps, but it cannot replace real demand.

My view

Medini can be a practical entry point into Iskandar Puteri.
