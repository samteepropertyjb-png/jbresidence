Horizon Hills Guide: Living, Schools, Horizon Mall and 2026 Home Prices

Sam Tee Property Advisor · REN 80322
Horizon Hills, Iskandar Puteri

New to Horizon Hills? This guide explains what the township is like, what is inside it, what homes cost in 2026 and what to check before you buy, so you can decide whether it belongs on your shortlist.

Quick answer

Horizon Hills is a mature, gated and guarded landed township in Iskandar Puteri, built around a golf course. It has schools and a neighbourhood mall inside, green surroundings and an active resale market. It suits families and buyers who want a settled community they can see today. Most homes are subsale, and in 2026 terrace homes start from about RM1 million.

Horizon Hills at a glance

  • Location: Iskandar Puteri, Johor Bahru, beside Bukit Indah and close to Eco Botanic and Bandar Wawari
  • Developer: Gamuda Land and UEM Sunrise (joint venture)
  • Size: about 1,200 acres, planned as 11 low-density residential precincts and about 6,000 homes
  • Tenure: freehold
  • Security: gated and guarded
  • Heart of the township: an 18-hole, par-72 golf course

What is inside Horizon Hills

The golf club

The Horizon Hills Golf & Country Club sits in the middle of the township. Even if you do not play golf, the fairways give the area open views and plenty of green space, and some homes look straight onto the course.

Schools

  • Invictus International School — a kindergarten-to-Year-12 campus at the township’s south entrance, on Persiaran Selatan.
  • SJK(C) Ming Chih — a Chinese-medium primary school inside Horizon Hills.

Having a primary school and an international school inside the township makes the daily school run easier for many families. For more options nearby, see international schools near Iskandar Puteri.

Horizon Mall and Horizon Square

Horizon Mall officially opened on 3 September 2026. It is a four-storey boutique lifestyle mall of about 150,000 sq ft, near the golf club and opposite Invictus International School. Grocery, food, fitness and family tenants cover daily needs, with Village Grocer among the shops, and it is open daily from 10am to 10pm. Next to it, Horizon Square adds 78 retail and commercial lots.

Before the mall opened, residents usually drove to Bukit Indah or Eco Botanic for daily shopping. Now more of those needs can be met inside the township. Read more in Horizon Mall & Horizon Hills property prices.

What living in Horizon Hills is like

  • Settled and green. Most precincts are already built and lived in, with mature trees, parks and lakes.
  • Quiet, low-density streets. The township is mainly landed homes rather than high-rise.
  • You will still drive. Like most of Iskandar Puteri, daily life is easier with a car, although the new mall reduces trips outside.
  • Different precincts, different feel. Golf views, canal views, street layout and distance to the gate all change daily life, so visit more than one precinct.

Horizon Hills home prices in 2026

Horizon Hills has terrace, cluster, semi-detached and bungalow homes. Most buyers look at subsale (resale) homes, because new phases are sold by the developer’s own sales team. A subsale home lets you inspect the real house, street and view, and move in sooner.

Buying a subsale home in Horizon Hills: what to check

  • Total cost, not just the asking price. Add renovation, repairs, furnishing, legal fees and loan costs.
  • House condition. Check the roof, waterproofing, wiring, plumbing and any extensions.
  • Precinct and position. Views, facing, traffic and distance to the gate affect daily life and resale value.
  • Land and built-up size. Compare the exact land size and floor plan, not only the house type.
  • Monthly costs. Ask about the security and maintenance charges for that precinct.
  • Title. Confirm the title details of the exact unit with your lawyer before signing.

Is Horizon Hills right for you?

It may suit you if you want a mature, guarded landed community, green surroundings, schools nearby and a home you can inspect before buying.

You may prefer somewhere else if you want a lower entry price for a similar house size or more shops within a short drive (compare with Eco Botanic), or if you would rather buy a brand-new home.

Frequently Asked Questions

Where is Horizon Hills?

Horizon Hills is a township in Iskandar Puteri, Johor Bahru. It sits beside Bukit Indah and is close to Eco Botanic and the newer Bandar Wawari area.

How much is a house in Horizon Hills in 2026?

As a rough 2026 subsale guide, terrace homes start from about RM1 million (up to RM1.6M–RM1.7M for corner, superlink or renovated units), cluster homes cost about RM1.4M–RM2.2M, semi-Ds about RM2M–RM3M and above, and bungalows from about RM4.5M.

Is Horizon Hills freehold?

Yes. Horizon Hills is a freehold township. As with any purchase, confirm the title of the exact house with your lawyer before you sign.

Is Horizon Hills gated and guarded?

Yes. Horizon Hills is a gated and guarded township with controlled entrances.

Which schools are inside Horizon Hills?

Invictus International School has a kindergarten-to-Year-12 campus at the township's south entrance, and SJK(C) Ming Chih is a Chinese-medium primary school inside Horizon Hills.

Is there a mall in Horizon Hills?

Yes. Horizon Mall officially opened on 3 September 2026. It is a four-storey boutique lifestyle mall with grocery, food, fitness and family tenants, open daily from 10am to 10pm.
