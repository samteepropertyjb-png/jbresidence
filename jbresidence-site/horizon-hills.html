Horizon Hills Guide: History, Schools, Horizon Mall and Buying a Subsale Home

Sam Tee Property Advisor · REN 80322
Horizon Hills, Iskandar Puteri

Horizon Hills is one of the best-known landed townships in Iskandar Puteri. It is a gated and guarded golf community with mature trees, a large resale market and, since 2026, its own neighbourhood mall. This guide explains how the township grew, what is inside it today, and how to buy a subsale home here.

Quick answer

Horizon Hills suits families and buyers who want a mature, guarded landed community with green surroundings, a golf club and schools inside the township. You pay for that maturity: homes are mostly subsale, and prices vary a lot by house type, precinct, land size, facing and condition.

How Horizon Hills grew

Horizon Hills was first launched in 2007. It is a 50:50 joint venture between Gamuda Land and UEM Sunrise, and it was Gamuda Land’s first township outside the Klang Valley.

The township covers about 1,200 acres of freehold land. It is planned as a low-density community of 11 residential precincts and about 6,000 homes when fully built. The design follows the natural hills of the site, with the golf course in the middle and homes, parks and lakes around it.

Close to 20 years after launch, most of the township is already lived in. That is the main difference between Horizon Hills and newer townships: you can see the finished streets, trees and neighbours before you buy.

What is inside Horizon Hills today

The golf club

The Horizon Hills Golf & Country Club sits at the heart of the township. It has an 18-hole, par-72 course, and some homes look out over the fairways. For golfers, this is one of the main reasons to choose Horizon Hills.

Schools

  • Invictus International School — a kindergarten-to-Year-12 campus at the township’s south entrance, on Persiaran Selatan.
  • SJK(C) Ming Chih — a Chinese-medium primary school inside Horizon Hills, within easy reach of many homes.

Having a primary school and an international school inside the township is a big plus for families. For more schools in the wider area, see the international schools near Iskandar Puteri guide.

Horizon Mall and Horizon Square

Horizon Mall officially opened on 3 September 2026. It is a four-storey boutique lifestyle mall of about 150,000 sq ft, near the golf club and opposite Invictus International School. Grocery, food, fitness and family tenants serve daily needs, with Village Grocer among the shops. The mall is open daily from 10am to 10pm.

Next to it, Horizon Square adds 78 retail and commercial lots. Before the mall, residents often drove to Bukit Indah or Eco Botanic for daily shopping. Now more of those needs can be met inside the township.

Homes in Horizon Hills: what you can buy

Horizon Hills has link houses, cluster homes, semi-detached houses and bungalows, plus some villa-style and high-rise products. Each precinct has its own character, so two houses at a similar price can feel very different.

Most buyers here are looking at subsale (resale) homes. New phases are sold by the developer’s own sales team. A subsale home lets you inspect the real house, street and view, and move in sooner.

Buying a subsale home in Horizon Hills: what to check

  • Total cost, not just the asking price. Add renovation, repairs, furnishing, legal fees and loan costs.
  • House condition. Check the roof, waterproofing, wiring, plumbing and any extensions.
  • Precinct and position. Golf or canal views, facing, traffic and distance to the gate all affect daily life and resale value.
  • Land and built-up size. Compare the exact land size and floor plan, not only the house type.
  • Monthly costs. Ask about the township security and maintenance charges for that precinct.
  • Title. Confirm the tenure and title details of the exact unit with your lawyer before signing.

Who may prefer somewhere else?

If you want a lower entry price for a similar house size, or more shops and food within a short drive, compare Horizon Hills with Eco Botanic. If you would rather buy a brand-new home and can wait for it to be built, look at new launches nearby.

Frequently Asked Questions

Who developed Horizon Hills?

Horizon Hills is a joint venture between Gamuda Land and UEM Sunrise. It was first launched in 2007 and was Gamuda Land's first township outside the Klang Valley.

Is Horizon Hills freehold?

Horizon Hills is described by its developer and major property portals as a freehold township. Always check the title of the exact house you plan to buy before you sign.

Which schools are inside Horizon Hills?

Invictus International School has a kindergarten-to-Year-12 campus at the township's south entrance, and SJK(C) Ming Chih is a Chinese-medium primary school inside Horizon Hills.

When did Horizon Mall open?

Horizon Mall officially opened on 3 September 2026. It is a four-storey boutique lifestyle mall in Horizon Hills and is open daily from 10am to 10pm.

Can I still buy a new home from the developer in Horizon Hills?

New phases are sold by the developer's own sales team. Most buyers who want a home they can inspect and move into sooner look at subsale (resale) homes, which is where I can help.
