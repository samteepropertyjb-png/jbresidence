Why shortlist it

A wider terrace layout in a new Iskandar Puteri growth area.

Ascent Park is a freehold strata landed project in Bandar Wawari. The main difference is its 25-foot frontage: compared with a common 22-foot terrace house, the living and dining zone can feel broader and less corridor-like. The trade-off is location maturity — buyers should be comfortable driving for daily needs while the surrounding township continues to develop.

Location and daily life

Useful for an Iskandar Puteri routine, but still car-dependent.

The project sits in Bandar Wawari near the international education corridor. It can make practical sense for households whose work, schools or regular activities are around EduCity, Medini, Puteri Harbour or the Second Link side of Iskandar Puteri. Test the actual weekday journey before deciding.

Future everyday convenience

The wider development also includes Ascent Hub, a planned commercial component intended to add neighbourhood convenience. This page focuses only on the landed homes; buyers should judge the house on today's access and their own routine rather than relying only on future amenities.

Buyer fit

A sensible option for the right household — not for everyone.

Worth considering if you...

  • want a new two-storey landed home with a wide frontage
  • need four bedrooms for family or multi-generation use
  • spend most of your time around Iskandar Puteri
  • can accept a developing township and drive for daily needs

Look elsewhere if you...

  • need mature shops and services within walking distance now
  • want a completed home that is ready for immediate move-in
  • prefer individual-title landed property rather than strata
  • are buying mainly for quick appreciation or guaranteed rental

Simple reason to compare it

If you like the wider 25-foot terrace format, compare the 25' × 60' and 25' × 70' plans side by side. The better choice depends on how much land depth, room space and monthly commitment your family will actually use.
