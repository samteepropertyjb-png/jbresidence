Why Medini for a serviced apartment?

Medini sits within the Iskandar Puteri zone of the Johor-Singapore Special Economic Zone (JS-SEZ). Unlike most other areas of Johor, Medini has historically offered foreign buyers full freehold ownership without a minimum purchase price restriction — making it one of the most accessible entry points for international buyers in the Iskandar region.

The Second Link (Tuas Checkpoint) is 9km away. With the RTS Link (Bukit Chagar–Woodlands North) opening in 2027, the cross-border commute from Medini to Singapore will become significantly easier for residents and tenants who work in Singapore.

Developer: Creed Group Japan

Bodaiju Residences is developed by Creed Property Malaysia Sdn. Bhd. — a wholly-owned subsidiary of Creed Group, a Japanese real estate group founded in 1996 by Toshihiko Mune. Creed Group has developed projects across Japan, Phnom Penh, Ho Chi Minh City, Bangkok, Jakarta, and Dhaka. Their Malaysia entry through Medini marks their first Johor project.

Japanese-developed property in Malaysia tends to carry a quality positioning premium — Creed's track record in Asia and the Bodaiju brand (named after the Bodhi tree, symbolising peace and longevity) reflects a deliberate lifestyle rather than volume-developer approach.

Who Bodaiju suits

  • Singapore-based buyers seeking a lower-entry-point Johor property with no minimum price restriction — RM 299,000 is approximately S$91,000 at mid-2026 exchange rates
  • Foreign buyers who want full ownership rights in Medini without the bureaucratic complexity of other zones
  • Investors looking for a managed rental product near the Second Link corridor, ahead of the RTS Link opening in 2027
  • Buyers on a RM 300K–700K budget who want a new launch in Iskandar Puteri rather than the Forest City highrise market

Who should look elsewhere

  • Families wanting freehold landed property — Bodaiju is leasehold serviced apartment; Eco Botanic or Horizon Hills are better fits
  • Buyers who prioritise international school proximity over Second Link access — Sunway City has SIS and other schools within walking distance
  • Buyers expecting short-term capital gains — like all new launches in the current market, Bodaiju is a medium-to-long hold

Sam's take

Bodaiju fills a real gap in the Medini market: a quality new-launch serviced apartment with no minimum price for foreign buyers, a credible Japanese developer, and genuine Second Link proximity. The entry price at RM 299,000 is the most accessible route into a new Iskandar Puteri project for foreign buyers. The dual-key units (only 5 remaining) are the most interesting for buyers who want flexibility — live in one half, rent out the other.

If your budget is RM 300K–700K, you want a new product (not resale), and the Second Link / JS-SEZ corridor makes sense for your profile, Bodaiju is worth a serious look. Get in touch and I'll walk you through the available units and floor plans.
