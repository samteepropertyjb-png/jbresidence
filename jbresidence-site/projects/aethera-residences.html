Aethera at a glance

Close enough to make the RTS part of the daily routine.

The project sits on Jalan Tun Abdul Razak in Johor Bahru. The official material presents a covered link bridge of about 400m to the Bukit Chagar RTS station, with JB Sentral, CIQ and City Square in the wider city-centre catchment.

The location is the feature.

Aethera is not positioned as a distant suburban condo with an RTS story attached. The proposed covered walking connection is central to the project. Before committing, buyers should still verify the final access point, route and operating arrangements.
