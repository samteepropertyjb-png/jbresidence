The bigger picture

A more complete landed-home lifestyle.

Riveria Garden brings together township scale, landed space and clubhouse facilities—three parts of daily life that are not always offered together in Iskandar Puteri.

Sam's takeWhy put this on your shortlist?

For buyers who want a landed home without giving up clubhouse living.

In Iskandar Puteri, many landed homes offer space, but fewer combine that landed lifestyle with a proper clubhouse. Riveria Garden also gives drivers practical route choices towards both the First Link and Second Link corridors.

If you like the privacy and space of a landed home, but still want a pool, gym, sports courts and shared community facilities, this is one project worth viewing and comparing.

The trade-off: Wawari is a newer township, so buyers should be comfortable with an area whose surrounding commercial conveniences are still developing.
