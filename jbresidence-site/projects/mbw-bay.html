Sam’s view

Worth comparing if the location fits your everyday life.

Consider this phase if you want an apartment in the Danga Bay corridor, prefer a smaller home and can work with an under-construction timeline. A study may help with working from home, but inspect its actual size and ventilation.

It may be a weaker fit if you need to move in soon, want a verified walk-to-station home, or need more bedrooms and storage. Compare maintenance charges, sinking fund, utilities and parking as well as the loan payment. Rental demand and future resale value are not guaranteed.

Timing matters: the October 2025 brochure shows an older 2027 estimate. The latest developer APDL checked on 21 October 2026 states December 2029 for Tower 1–3. Confirm the contractual delivery date before paying a booking fee.
