Sam’s view

A little more travel. More room for a seaside weekend.

If you would rather avoid paying a higher price per square foot just to be beside CIQ or the RTS, and you are comfortable spending a little longer on the journey, MBW Bay is worth considering.

For buyers who prefer to spend their weekends away from the busy station area, this Danga Bay address offers a different setting to explore. Picture a sea-facing apartment where you can slow down and enjoy your time off. The key is finding a unit whose view, layout and price make sense for you.

The trade-off is travel time. Compare the actual price per square foot of your shortlisted units and try your daily route. Sea views depend on the selected tower, floor and facing; check current and planned buildings too. Visit on a weekend to judge how the surroundings feel to you.
