New Casa Suites @ Mercu 3 is Phase 3 of R&F Princess Cove in Tanjung Puteri. The reason to shortlist it is straightforward: it places daily city convenience and the JB–Singapore commute at the centre of the buying decision. The right question is not whether it is “the best” project, but whether this location and high-rise lifestyle match how you will actually use the home.

Who should consider it — and who should compare elsewhere?

It may suit you if…

  • You travel through JB CIQ regularly and want a home designed around a more walkable city routine.
  • You prefer having retail, dining and facilities within an integrated high-rise precinct.
  • You want choices ranging from a compact studio to a family-sized four-bedroom layout.
  • You understand that the right stack, view and layout matter more than choosing the project name alone.

You should compare elsewhere if…

  • You want landed space, a private garden or a quieter low-density neighbourhood.
  • You rarely use JB CIQ, so you may be paying for a location advantage you do not need.
  • You are sensitive to city traffic, activity, construction phases or high-rise community density.
  • Your purchase only works if a specific rent or capital gain is guaranteed — neither should be assumed.
The real location proposition

For buyers who put the JB–Singapore commute first

The brochure states a roughly 650-metre sheltered connection toward JB CIQ. Bukit Chagar RTS station is the future cross-border rail anchor in this area, but buyers should separate the existing walking connection from the RTS service itself. As of September 2026, testing and final preparations are ongoing; actual passenger operations, walking time and daily experience should be checked again before purchase.

Choose by daily use, not only size

Unit types from studio to four bedrooms

A smaller layout may fit an occasional city base, while a larger two- or three-bedroom plan may be more practical for regular own stay. Compare usable rooms, storage, orientation and how often family members will stay — not only the price per square foot.

The trade-offs matter

What I would verify before recommending a unit

The location can be strong without every unit being equally suitable.

I would first clarify your own-stay frequency, border-crossing routine, household size and holding budget. Then we compare the current unit list by layout efficiency, facing, floor, likely noise exposure, parking and total purchase cost. For an investment purchase, we also test the numbers using a conservative rent assumption rather than treating rental demand as guaranteed.

Project-level checks

  • Current construction and completion information in the sale documents.
  • Final facility schedule, maintenance charges and house rules.
  • Actual walking route to JB CIQ and how it feels at your normal travel hour.
  • Density across the wider precinct and future phases nearby.

Unit-level checks

  • Usable bedroom and living-room proportions, not brochure area alone.
  • Stack orientation, view protection and possible traffic or city noise.
  • Parking allocation, furnishing package and all payable costs.
  • Whether the layout still works if your family or usage changes.
