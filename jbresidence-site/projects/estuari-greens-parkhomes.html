Why consider it?

A new landed-home choice for buyers who already like Puteri Harbour.

The appeal is simple: after a long quiet period for new landed launches in Puteri Harbour, buyers can now compare a lower-entry terrace with a wider Semi-D in the same neighbourhood.

The main trade-off

Estuari is still maturing. Shops, landscaping, road links and surrounding phases will take time to feel complete, so this is a better fit for patient own-stay buyers than anyone expecting fully established convenience immediately.

Daily life & location

Green space, sports and a future link towards Bandar Wawari.

The wider Estuari neighbourhood sits near the existing Estuari Sports Centre on the Puteri Harbour side of Iskandar Puteri. The project briefing also shows a new access road connecting the Estuari side towards Bandar Wawari. When completed, it should improve local connectivity and support the area's gradual development.

  • Estuari Sports Centre with swimming, racket sports and gym facilities
  • Near EduCity and international-school options
  • Access towards Puteri Harbour, Medini and Gleneagles Hospital
  • Approximately 16km from the Malaysia-Singapore Second Link

Buyer fit

A good shortlist for some families - not a universal recommendation.

Worth considering if you...

  • want a freehold landed home for long-term own stay
  • need four bedrooms or multi-generational flexibility
  • prefer more land and built-up space over mature retail convenience
  • work, study or spend most of your time around Iskandar Puteri

Look elsewhere if you...

  • need established shops and dining within walking distance now
  • commute to central Johor Bahru every day and dislike longer drives
  • want a low-maintenance lock-and-leave home
  • will not use the extra rooms, garden or larger ownership budget
