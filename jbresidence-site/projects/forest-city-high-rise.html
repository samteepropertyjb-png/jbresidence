Location

The apartments are within Forest City, a waterfront master-planned area in Gelang Patah near the Malaysia-Singapore Second Link corridor. The setting is different from a central Johor Bahru condominium: it is quieter and more resort-oriented, so the right choice depends on how often you travel, where your daily activities are and whether you are comfortable driving.

There are six sizes to compare. Y225, Y076 and Y221 are the three practical starting choices I would show first to many MM2H applicants because they cover a compact 1+1 room, a larger 1+1 room and a two-room layout without immediately moving into the larger price range.
