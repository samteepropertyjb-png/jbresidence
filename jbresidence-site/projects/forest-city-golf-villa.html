Location

The villas sit within Forest City Golf Resort in Gelang Patah, close to the Second Link corridor. The location makes the most sense for buyers whose Singapore travel is centred on Tuas and who prefer a quieter resort environment over central Johor Bahru.

Forest City Golf Resort is approximately 5 km from the Second Link corridor. Actual travel time depends on the selected route, immigration traffic and time of day, so I recommend testing the drive based on your normal routine.

V120 and V160

V120 is the more compact two-bedroom option. V160 adds a third bedroom, a larger master suite and two open terraces for buyers who need more space.
