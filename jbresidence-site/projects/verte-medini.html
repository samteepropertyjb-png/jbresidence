Project overview

Straightforward project information for your Medini comparison.

Located along Persiaran Medini Utara 3, Verte Medini has four apartment towers over 4.32 acres. Towers A and B have 313 homes each; Towers C and D have 240 homes each. Facilities are planned at Level 8.

Included project details

The main points to check alongside the unit layout.

Parking

Parking allocation varies by unit type and level. Ask for the exact assigned bays and whether the allocation is normal or tandem for your chosen unit.

Partial furnishing

The stated package includes kitchen cabinets, hob and hood, dryer, and installed air-conditioning. Quantities vary by unit type.

Facilities

Planned facilities include a swimming pool, fitness room, children’s playground, multipurpose hall, BBQ gazebo, jogging track and garden pavilion.

Medini tenure

Private Lease Scheme: request the documents early.

The project is under Medini’s Private Lease Scheme. The sales material states a lease term to 1 August 2142 after extension. The SPA and financing treatment should be checked for the exact purchase with the appointed professionals and bank.

  • Leasehold under the Medini Private Lease Scheme
  • Maintenance fee stated at RM0.35 per sq ft, including sinking fund
  • Foreign-buyer eligibility and current state charges should be confirmed for the chosen unit
